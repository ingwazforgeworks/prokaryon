HALF-SIZE EDITION
Each sheet is half the original pixel width and height, using nearest-neighbor sampling. Odd source dimensions are rounded down by half a pixel. Sprite order, labels and gene mappings are unchanged.

PROKARYON — 86 GENE PROTEIN CONCEPTS

Concept art based on the user-provided 86-gene list. Structures and localization are proposed visual choices, not experimentally determined folds or edits to the vault. R=membrane sensory receptor; I=intracellular soluble sensor/receptor; M=membrane protein/apparatus, not sensory receptor; P=other protein, no bilayer in this art. Some sprites represent assemblies rather than individual polypeptides. Sheets are labeled concept atlases, not validated pixel-perfect engine assets.

CLASSIFICATION LEGEND
R: Membrane sensory receptor
I: Soluble intracellular sensor/receptor
M: Membrane protein/apparatus; not sensory receptor
P: Other protein; no bilayer depicted

READING THE SHEETS
Rows and columns are numbered from 1, top-left. Labels identify genes.
Artwork is generated concept art, not experimentally determined structure.
A lipid bilayer means membrane localization, not necessarily receptor function.
PCHR, RDXN and QURR are depicted as soluble sensors; PHVC is a membrane sensor.
All localization choices are provisional art direction based on the provided names.
No vault files have been edited.
These are labeled PNG atlases, not individually cropped or pixel-grid-validated sprites.
Some apparatus/structural genes use assembled complexes as their icon.
