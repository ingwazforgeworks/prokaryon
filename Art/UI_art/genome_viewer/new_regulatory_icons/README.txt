Prokaryon unified localization icons

Ten icons, each in 32x32 and 64x64 PNG.
Foreground: #BBD0D5. Transparency: binary alpha.
64x64 icons are exact nearest-neighbor 2x copies of the 32x32 originals.
Spritesheets: one row, no spacing, in the following order:
0: polar
1: antipolar
2: bipolar
3: lateral
4: bilateral
5: antilateral
6: secreted
7: transmembrane
8: cytosolic
9: membrane_anchored

Lateral = left side; antilateral = right side.
Polar = top pole; antipolar = bottom pole.
Membrane anchored shows an externally tethered protein.
