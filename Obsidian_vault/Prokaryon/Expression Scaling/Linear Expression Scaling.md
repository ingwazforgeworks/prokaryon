\(F(E)=aE\)

Output is directly proportional to expressed abundance, with no ceiling. The default for enzymes whose substrate is the limiting factor rather than the enzyme itself.

**Used by:** [[Fluxidase (FLUX)]], [[ATP Synthase (ATPS)]], [[Anabolase (ANAB)]], [[Reducin Dehydrogenase (RDCD)]], most permeases

**Caution:** unbounded output means the only thing stopping runaway expression is [[Expression Capacity]] and resource limitation. A gene using linear scaling with a cheap input is a balance risk.
