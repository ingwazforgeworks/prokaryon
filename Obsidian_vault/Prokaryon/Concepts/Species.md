**Concept:** What the player owns and names  
**Status:** Confirmed — requirements V11 and V18

A species is the unit of ownership, identity and genetic change. Individual cells are expendable; the species is what persists, what the player names, and what an edit applies to.

## Edits are species-wide

Requirement V18 confirms whole-species edits, and the backlog treats this as settled: a committed edit applies to every cell of the species after a mandatory delay, including cells born during maturation and cells living in completely different conditions.

That has a consequence worth stating plainly. The player cannot tune one cell for the cave and another for open water — they change what the species *is*, and then watch how one genome performs across every niche it occupies. Specification §3.1 decision D02 compared focal-cell-only, descendants-only, and all-owned-cells editing; species-wide is the confirmed answer, and it is what makes [[Regulation]] load-bearing. Conditional expression is the only way one genome behaves differently in two places.

Backlog task S1-16 owns species-wide adoption across different local environments.

## Identity through transfer

Requirement V11 promises stable ownership and naming, including after genetic exchange. Specification §8.2 decision D25 has not chosen between owner species only, owner species plus strains, and additional genetic clustering — but it sets one hard constraint: a genetic classification must not silently replace the named player species.

Specification §8.3 adds a related obligation: preserve social attribution without implying that one player owns a real biological gene. [[Competence Uptake (COMP)]] strains this hardest, because a gene absorbed from an anonymous corpse has no donor to credit. See [[Ectodin]].

## Strains — D10

Specification §3.1 asks whether lineages can diverge simultaneously: one active genome is simple but limits experimentation; several strains enable niche specialisation with more interface and balance load; emergent mutation adds evolutionary flavour and unpredictable maintenance. Unresolved.
