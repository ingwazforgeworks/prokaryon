**Concept:** A locally distinct habitat, and the contract every one of them must satisfy  
**Status:** Confirmed as a requirement — V09 names microniches explicitly

A microniche is a place where different genes are correct. If a cave favours exactly the same genome as open water, it is decoration.

## The niche contract

Specification §7.2 requires each niche to be authored against these seven points. A niche missing any of them is not finished.

| Point | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| Environmental signature and geometry | What is measurably different here? |
| Resource sources, sinks, transport boundaries | Where does material enter and leave? |
| Favoured and disadvantaged strategies | Which genes win here and which are dead weight? |
| Route for a non-steered cell to enter, remain, leave | Can a cell get here without being driven? |
| Capacity and failure modes | What happens when it is overcrowded? |
| What other species can change about it | Is it modifiable by play? |
| Visual and diagnostic cues | Can a player tell what kind of place this is? |

The fourth point is the hard one, and it is a hard requirement. Specification §7.2 states that success must not depend on precise manual movement the player is forbidden to perform, so a niche reachable only by steering is broken regardless of how interesting it is inside.

## What makes a niche different

Specification §7.2 notes a microcave does not need every effect at once. The available levers:

- Reduced [[Flow]], which retains secretions and starves the interior of [[Oxidex]]
- Attenuated [[Light]], removing [[Phototrophy]] and reducing photodamage
- Nutrient trapping, which makes public goods defensible
- Restricted entrances, limiting both immigration and escape
- Protected surfaces for attachment — see [[Surfaces and Geometry]]
- Local waste buildup, raising [[pH]] and [[Fermentate]]

## Niches players build

The interesting cases are not authored. Two genes create habitat:

- [[Matrixin Synthase (MTXS)]] builds a biofilm whose interior develops its own [[Redox Stratification]] and acidity gradient.
- [[Protopump (PPMP)]] acidifies a colony's surroundings, excluding competitors without [[Acid Tolerase (ACDT)]].

Specification §7.4's emergence gate asks for two different outcomes from the same rules under different conditions, with the causal mechanism explained. A biofilm that becomes anoxic in still water and stays oxidised in current is that demonstration.

## How a cell arrives without being steered

The permitted routes, all physiological:

| Route | Gene |
|---|---|
| Carried by current into a trap | none — [[Flow]] does it |
| Gradient-following into better conditions | [[Taxis Regulator (TAXR)]] plus a receptor |
| Passive drift along the depth axis | [[Buoyin (BUOY)]] |
| Sticking on arrival and staying | [[Adhesin (ADHN)]] |
| Born there | [[Division]] |
