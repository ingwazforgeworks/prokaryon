**Field type:** Scalar concentration and derived osmotic stress  
**Status:** Confirmed — requirement V09  
**Sensed by:** [[Osmoreceptor (OSMR)]] (indirectly, through membrane tension)  
**Adapted to by:** [[Osmolyn Synthase (OSMS)]], [[Aquaporin (AQUP)]]

How much dissolved salt the water holds, and therefore which way water moves across a cell's membrane. Salinity does not kill outright; it taxes.

## Gameplay role

Salinity is the field that makes dispersal expensive. Because crossing a gradient needs a fast response and living beyond it needs a sustained one, a lineage must buy two genes to move between zones — and one of them is useless once it arrives.

| Timescale | Gene | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Immediate | [[Aquaporin (AQUP)]] | Gates water flux; survives the shock, buffers nothing |
| Sustained | [[Osmolyn Synthase (OSMS)]] | Accumulates [[Osmolyn]]; holds a new steady state |

That double cost is what turns a salinity boundary into a real barrier rather than a coloured region, and it is why separate populations either side of one can diverge — see [[Microniche]].

## A carbon tax, not an energy tax

[[Osmolyn]] is built from carbon that will never become [[Biomass]]. A cell in saline water therefore grows slowly even when perfectly well fed, which is a different failure shape from every other stressor in [[Homeostasis]]: the cell is not damaged, not short of [[ATP]], and still cannot compete.

## Sensing the symptom

[[Osmoreceptor (OSMR)]] reports membrane tension, not salinity. High tension means trouble but does not distinguish salty water from osmotic overshoot caused by the cell's own [[Osmolyn]] production — so a correction can trigger the receptor that drove it. Specification §2.3 requires deliberate simplifications to be recorded with their consequence; this feedback loop is the consequence, and it is intentionally left in place to give promoter hysteresis something real to solve.

## Open — decision from §7.1

Is osmotic adaptation reversible expression or long-term specialisation? This vault assumes reversible, which keeps the promoter decision meaningful. Permanent specialisation would move the content into [[Morphology]] and delete the decision — see [[Osmolyn]].
