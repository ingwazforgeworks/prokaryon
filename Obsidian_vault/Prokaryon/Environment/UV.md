**Field Name:** UV  
**Status:** Proposed — **not in the confirmed environmental field list.** See the scope note below.  
**Varies with:** Depth and shading. Strongest at the surface, effectively absent below a shallow layer.  
**Sensed by:** No dedicated receptor — only indirectly, through [[Damage Receptor (DMGR)]]

High-energy light that damages the genome rather than feeding the cell. UV carries no usable energy in this design; it only costs.

### What makes it different from [[Light]]

Every other field is a trade. UV is not: there is no amount of it a cell benefits from, and nothing in the [[Gene Catalog]] harvests it.

That makes it a pure gradient of hazard laid over the same water that holds the most [[Light]] and the most [[Oxidex]]. The surface becomes the most productive and most dangerous place to live, and the depth at which a lineage settles becomes a real decision instead of a preference.

### The genes that answer it

| Gene | Role |
|---|---|
| [[Repairase (REPR)]] | Repairs the damage after it happens; continuous cost |
| [[Damage Receptor (DMGR)]] | Detects accumulated damage, so a cell can respond to UV without sensing it |
| [[Photoprotectin (PHPR)]] | Partial shielding, via [[Carotin]] |
| [[Capsulin Synthase (CAPS)]] | An outer layer that absorbs some of it |
| [[Buoyin (BUOY)]] | Avoidance — the cheapest answer, if the cell can afford the volume |

### You cannot see it coming

There is no UV receptor, deliberately. A cell learns about UV only after being damaged, through [[Damage Receptor (DMGR)]], which makes this the one field where reactive response is the *only* option and preparation must be either constitutive or based on a proxy.

The proxy is [[Photochrome (PCHR)]]: UV correlates with visible light, so a cell can infer exposure from a band it can actually detect. That inference is a genuine piece of player reasoning rather than a rule, and it is the kind of thing confirmed requirement V26 is meant to encourage — the cell is not told about UV, but a clever genome acts as though it knows.

### Interaction with mutation

If UV damage also raises mutation rate, the surface becomes both the most dangerous place and the fastest-evolving one. That is an attractive idea and an economically risky one, because it couples a hazard to [[Mutation Points]] and could make deliberate self-irradiation optimal. Unresolved; see decisions D13–D16.
