**Field type:** 2D velocity field, authored or procedurally generated  
**Status:** Confirmed — requirement V09  
**Sensed by:** Nothing directly — no gene reads the current  
**Resisted by:** [[Adhesin (ADHN)]], [[Pilin (PILN)]], [[Matrixin]]  
**Used by:** [[Buoyin (BUOY)]]

Water movement. Flow carries every [[Soluble Resource]], delivers [[Oxidex]] into enclosed spaces, disperses cells that cannot hold on, and strips away everything a cell secretes.

## It is the only field with no receptor

Deliberately. A cell cannot measure the current, so it cannot regulate against it — it can only be built to resist or accept it. That makes flow the purest environmental pressure in the game: no sensing gene helps, and the only responses are structural.

This is also what makes flow the main enforcer of requirement V02. A cell does not get swept somewhere because the player failed to react; it gets swept because of what it is.

## What flow does to each strategy

| Strategy | Effect of strong flow |
|---|---|
| Free-swimming | Position is decided by the current, not by [[Flagellar Motor Protein (FLGM)]] |
| Attached, via [[Adhesin (ADHN)]] | Holds position; must eat what arrives |
| Public-goods producer | Ruined — [[Siderin]] and hydrolase products are carried away before use |
| Biofilm, via [[Matrixin]] | Protected; the matrix slows flow locally |
| Aerobic, via [[Oxidex Reductase (OXDR)]] | Favoured — flow is what replenishes [[Oxidex]] |

Flow therefore sets which of the two halves of [[Morphology]] is viable: cooperation needs slow water, because diffusion and advection are what decide whether a secreted molecule benefits its producer.

## It creates the anoxic world

[[Redox Stratification]] exists because flow is uneven. Open channels are flushed and oxidising; cave interiors and biofilm centres are not. Without a flow field, [[Sulfex Reductase (SLFR)]] and the entire dark-niche branch of the catalog would have nowhere to live.

## Open questions

- **Must organisms alter flow?** Specification §7.1 asks this directly. [[Matrixin]] currently does, locally. Whether large colonies reshape the field at scale is undecided and expensive.
- Is the field authored per habitat or generated? Specification §7.3 decision leaves this with world structure.
