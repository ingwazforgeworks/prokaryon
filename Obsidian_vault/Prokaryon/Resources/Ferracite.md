**Resource Name:** Ferracite  
**Type:** [[Insoluble Resource]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Iron-bearing mineral rock. Abundant, immobile and almost inert — the iron within it is not chemically available until a cell either reduces it in place or sends out a chelator to prise it loose.  
**Source:** The substrate itself. Effectively unlimited where present.  
**Uses:** Reduced in place by [[Ferracite Reductase (FCTR)]], or chelated at range by secreted [[Siderin]], in both cases releasing [[Ferron]].  
**Strategic role:** The one insoluble stock that never runs out, which makes it the anchor of a sessile strategy. A surface colony on Ferracite has a permanent food supply and must instead solve crowding, [[Flow]], predation and waste buildup — see [[Microniche]].
