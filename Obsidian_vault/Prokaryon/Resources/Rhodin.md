**Resource Name:** Rhodin  
**Type:** Pigment — cell-internal  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Absorbs:** Mid-spectrum (green-yellow) light  
**Description:** A small, cheap pigment built around a light-isomerising chain. The least expensive pigment in the game and the only one that can drive a pump directly rather than feeding a reaction centre.  
**Source:** [[Rhodin Synthase (RHDS)]]  
**Uses:** Required by [[Photorhodin (PHOR)]]; enables a mid-spectrum [[Photochrome (PCHR)]].

**The starter pigment.** Rhodin is where a lineage begins with [[Light]], because one cheap pigment plus one cheap pump produces usable [[Fluxin]] with no reaction centre and no [[Reducin]]. It is also where that lineage stops: see [[Photorhodin (PHOR)]] on why energy without carbon is a trap.

**It sees what the others miss.** Because Rhodin absorbs the middle of the spectrum rather than the ends, a Rhodin cell can sense and use light in water where [[Chlorin]] lineages have already stripped out the red and blue. See [[Light]] on spectral competition.
