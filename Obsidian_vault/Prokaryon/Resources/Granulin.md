**Resource Name:** Granulin  
**Type:** [[Insoluble Resource]] — internal store  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** An insoluble polymer the cell builds inside itself from surplus [[Glycon]]. Unlike every other insoluble stock, Granulin sits within the membrane, so no competitor can reach it while its owner is alive.  
**Source:** Polymerised from intracellular [[Glycon]] by [[Granulin Synthase (GRNS)]].  
**Uses:** Depolymerised back into [[Glycon]] by [[Granulin Hydrolase (GRNH)]] when uptake falls short of demand.

**Storing is always a loss.** Both conversions waste material, so a cell that stores and immediately retrieves ends up poorer than one that never stored. Granulin only pays across a genuine famine, which makes it a bet about the future rather than an upgrade — and a bet the player places by choosing a promoter condition, not by clicking at the right moment.

**It occupies the body.** Granulin takes up internal volume, so a heavily-stocked cell is larger without being closer to division, interacting with [[Size Regulator (SIZR)]] and with drag in [[Flow]]. A full larder is a slower cell.

**Death reveals it.** Stored Granulin returns to the world as [[Carbohydron]] when its owner dies, so a well-provisioned cell is a richer corpse. This is the one case where investing in survival directly subsidises predators and scavengers, and [[Bdellase (BDEL)]] lineages should prefer well-fed prey for exactly that reason.
