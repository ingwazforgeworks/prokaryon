**Resource Name:** Ectodin  
**Type:** [[Soluble Resource]] — environmental genetic material  
**Status:** Open — depends on decisions D20–D24  
**Description:** Fragments of genetic material released into the water when cells die and lyse. Ectodin degrades over time, so a habitat's environmental gene pool reflects what has recently died there rather than everything that ever lived.  
**Source:** Cell death and lysis, including kills by [[Lysin]] and [[Bdellase (BDEL)]].  
**Uses:** Taken up by [[Competence Uptake (COMP)]], potentially supplying genes their recipient never unlocked.

**Transformation's resource.** Ectodin is what makes the transformation route in [[Horizontal Gene Transfer]] a genuinely different mechanic from conjugation rather than a reskin. Conjugation is a deliberate exchange between two living, consenting-or-not cells; Ectodin scavenging is opportunistic, untraceable, and requires nothing from the donor except that it died nearby.

**Unresolved provenance.** Specification §8.1 notes that transformation makes provenance harder and lets genes spread without deliberate trade. That cuts against requirement V11's promise of stable species ownership and attribution, so several decisions must land before this resource can ship:

| Decision | Question it raises for Ectodin |
|---|---|
| D22 | Can a cell absorb DNA from an incompatible lineage? |
| D23 | Can Ectodin carry a harmful gene? |
| D24 | Does an absorbed gene reach only the recipient, or the whole species? |
| D25 | Who is credited when a gene arrives from an anonymous corpse? |

**Anti-abuse concern.** Specification §8.3 requires testing exchange loops between alternate accounts. A player who can kill their own cells to seed Ectodin, and absorb it with another species they own, has a laundering route for genes — so any implementation needs the same auditing as conjugation, without a donor to audit.
