**Resource Name:** Glycon  
**Type:** Soluble nutrient  
**Description:** A dissolved carbohydrate-like fuel that cells can absorb and metabolize. Glycon released from deposits is available to any nearby cell with suitable uptake machinery.  
**Source:** General environment; Released from [[Carbohydron]] by [[Glycon Hydrolase (GLYH)]].  
**Uses:** Imported by [[Glycon Permease (GLYP)]] and converted into [[Fluxin]] by [[Fluxidase (FLUX)]].
