**Resource Name:** Ferron  
**Type:** [[Soluble Resource]] — electron donor  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Reduced iron and other redox-active mineral constituents. A mineral-associated fuel concentrated around surfaces and deposits rather than distributed through open water, it rewards attachment and specialised extraction machinery.  
**Source:** Liberated from [[Ferracite]] by [[Ferracite Reductase (FCTR)]] at the mineral surface, or freed at a distance by secreted [[Siderin]].  
**Uses:** Imported by [[Ferron Permease (FERP)]] and converted into [[Fluxin]] by [[Fluxidase (FLUX)]].  
**Strategic role:** The only fuel that is fundamentally tied to a location. Ferron does not drift to the cell, so a Ferron lineage must solve *staying put* before it can solve eating — which pulls in [[Adhesin (ADHN)]] or [[Pilin (PILN)]] and makes [[Surfaces and Geometry]] matter mechanically rather than decoratively.

Two extraction routes exist and they differ in who benefits. [[Ferracite Reductase (FCTR)]] works only where the cell is physically touching mineral and keeps the product close. [[Siderin Synthase (SIDS)]] works at range but releases a public good that any neighbour with a permease can harvest.
