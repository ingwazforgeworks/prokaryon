**Resource Name:** Carotin  
**Type:** Pigment — cell-internal  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Absorbs:** Blue-violet light; dissipates excess energy as heat  
**Description:** A long unsaturated chain that does two jobs badly enough to be interesting: it harvests blue light into a reaction centre, and it safely discards energy the cell cannot use.  
**Source:** [[Carotin Synthase (CRTS)]]  
**Uses:** Required by [[Photoprotectin (PHPR)]]; acts as an accessory antenna for [[Reaction Center (RXNC)]]; enables a blue-sensing [[Photochrome (PCHR)]].

**The dual role is the point.** Carotin is simultaneously an antenna and a safety valve, so expressing more of it both increases harvest and increases the cell's ability to survive harvest. That makes it the only pigment without a clean optimum — and the reason [[Photoprotectin (PHPR)]] is a pigment-dependent gene rather than a standalone one.

**It is what makes bright water survivable.** A [[Chlorin]] lineage in full [[Light]] captures more energy than its reaction centres can process, and the excess becomes damage. Carotin is the difference between a phototroph that can live at the surface and one confined to depth.

**Visible consequence.** Carotin-rich cells read as warm-coloured. Since [[Chlorin]] cells read cool, pigment composition tells a player what a cell is doing and roughly where it expects to live, with no overlay needed — the cheapest diagnostic available under specification §10.5.
