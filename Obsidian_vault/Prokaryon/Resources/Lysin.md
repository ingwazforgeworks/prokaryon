**Resource Name:** Lysin  
**Type:** [[Soluble Resource]] — diffusible toxin  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** A secreted agent that damages cell envelopes. It diffuses, decays, and does not distinguish between targets: everything susceptible within range is harmed, including the producer's own kin.  
**Source:** Secreted by [[Lysin Synthase (LYSS)]].  
**Uses:** Inflicts damage on cells lacking [[Lysin Resistase (LYSR)]]. Kills, and in doing so produces [[Cerumen]], [[Carbohydron]] and [[Ectodin]] for whatever is still alive.

**Dose-response, not a hit.** Lysin is a field, so its effect depends on concentration and duration rather than on a targeting decision. Backlog task S3-10 treats dose-response and tolerance as the model, which means a thin cloud is a growth penalty and a thick one is lethal — and a cell can walk out of it if it is motile.

**A toxin strategy costs two genes.** Because Lysin harms kin, a producer must also express [[Lysin Resistase (LYSR)]] or poison its own colony. Specification §7.4 requires that range, specificity, cost, and resistant competitors all matter, and paying twice before gaining anything is how that is enforced. A resistant competitor makes the whole investment worthless in that neighbourhood.

**It feeds the neighbours.** Killing cells releases their contents. A pure toxin lineage without good uptake genes performs the work and other species eat the corpses, which makes aggression a poor standalone strategy and a strong complement to scavenging.

**Entry route matters.** A thin envelope carrying many permeases admits Lysin readily; [[Envelope Thickener (ENVT)]] and [[Capsulin]] slow it. The fastest eater in the game is also the most exposed to the cheapest attack, which is the same tradeoff [[Envelope Thickener (ENVT)]] describes from the other side.
