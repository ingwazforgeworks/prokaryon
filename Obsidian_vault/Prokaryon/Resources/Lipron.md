**Resource Name:** Lipron  
**Type:** [[Soluble Resource]] — electron donor  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Lipids, proteins and complex organic matter. Concentrated biological food, abundant in dead cells and debris, and energy-dense per unit imported. It requires more processing machinery than [[Glycon]], and extracellular digestion releases usable material that nearby scavengers can take.  
**Source:** Released from [[Cerumen]] by [[Lipron Hydrolase (LIPH)]]; accumulates wherever cells have died.  
**Uses:** Imported by [[Lipron Permease (LIPP)]], converted into [[Fluxin]] by [[Fluxidase (FLUX)]], and incorporated into [[Biomass]] by [[Anabolase (ANAB)]].  
**Strategic role:** The scavenger's fuel. Lipron abundance tracks mortality rather than the environment, so a lineage that eats it depends on other species dying nearby — a dependency that collapses in clean, sparse water.
