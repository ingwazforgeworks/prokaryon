**Resource Name:** Capsulin  
**Type:** Cell-surface polymer  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** A dense polymer layer laid down on the producer's own surface. Chemically similar to [[Matrixin]] but never released: it remains bound to the cell that built it and is carried with it.  
**Source:** Built by [[Capsulin Synthase (CAPS)]] from carbon and [[ATP]].  
**Uses:** Impedes [[Bdellase (BDEL)]] attachment, blunts [[Lysin]] exposure, and slows desiccation and osmotic shock.

**Private protection.** Capsulin is the deliberate counterpart to [[Matrixin]]. Both cost carbon and both defend against the same threats, but one shelters everybody nearby and the other shelters nobody but its owner:

| | Capsulin | [[Matrixin]] |
|---|---|---|
| Who it protects | The producer only | Everything inside it |
| Exploitable | No | Yes |
| Cost per cell protected | Full | Shared, if neighbours also produce |
| Survives the producer's death | No | Yes |

A lineage under heavy predation can therefore choose between paying alone and reliably, or paying less in a group that cheats can join. Specification §1.3 asks that each adaptation solve a problem while creating a cost or vulnerability, and here the vulnerability is economic rather than physical.

**Cost of thickness.** A capsule sits between the cell and the water, so it also slows uptake — the same tradeoff as [[Envelope Thickener (ENVT)]], and the two stack badly.
