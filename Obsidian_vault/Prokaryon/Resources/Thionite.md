**Resource Name:** Thionite  
**Type:** [[Insoluble Resource]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** A brittle sulfide mineral crust that forms on rock and around seeps. Chemically rich but physically locked: the bonds holding its sulfur are stronger than a membrane transporter can act on.  
**Source:** Mineral surfaces, cave walls and seep margins. Does not drift.  
**Uses:** Dissolved by [[Thionite Lyase (THNL)]] to release [[Sulfex]].  
**Strategic role:** A fixed, non-renewing fuel deposit. Unlike [[Carbohydron]] and [[Cerumen]], which are replenished by death, Thionite is finite per location — so a colony that mines it out must move, and attachment genes decide whether it can.
