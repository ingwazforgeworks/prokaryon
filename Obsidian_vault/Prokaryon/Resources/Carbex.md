**Resource Name:** Carbex  
**Type:** [[Soluble Resource]] — inorganic carbon  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Dissolved inorganic carbon. Chemically the cheapest carbon in the world and energetically the most expensive to use, because it arrives fully oxidised and must be reduced before it can become anything.  
**Source:** Ambient and abundant almost everywhere; released by every respiring cell as a byproduct.  
**Uses:** Imported by [[Carbex Permease (CBXP)]] and fixed into [[Glycon]] by [[Glycon Synthase (GLYS)]], which also requires [[Reducin]] and [[ATP]].

**Abundance without ease.** Carbex is the only fuel-adjacent resource a lineage is unlikely to run out of, which is precisely why it is gated behind the longest dependency chain in the [[Gene Catalog]]. Autotrophy trades scarcity for cost: unlimited carbon in exchange for four genes, continuous [[Reducin]] production, and an energy budget permanently diverted away from growth.

**Acidity coupling.** Dissolved inorganic carbon and [[pH]] are chemically linked, so a dense colony's respiration raises Carbex and lowers pH together. Specification §2.4 requires that pH be handled logarithmically and never averaged as a concentration, so this coupling is modelled as a declared approximation rather than real carbonate chemistry.
