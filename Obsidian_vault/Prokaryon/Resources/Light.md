**Resource Name:** Light  
**Type:** External energy field  
**Status:** Confirmed — requirement V09  
**Description:** An energy input rather than a substance. Light has an intensity and a direction, is attenuated by depth, occlusion and crowding, and becomes damaging above the level a cell can use.  
**Source:** External to the simulation, which carries no conservation obligation. Four proposed genes also add a local contribution, spent from [[ATP]]: [[Red Fluorescent Protein (RFP)]], [[Green Fluorescent Protein (GFP)]], [[Yellow Fluorescent Protein (YFP)]] and [[Blue Fluorescent Protein (BFP)]]. Emitted light is weak beside environmental light, and it falls off with distance.  
**Uses:** Sensed by [[Photochrome (PCHR)]] and [[Photovector (PHVC)]]. Converted into [[Fluxin]] by [[Photorhodin (PHOR)]], or into [[Fluxin]] and [[Reducin]] by [[Reaction Center (RXNC)]] when [[Chlorin]] is present.  
**Damage:** Excess light injures the cell unless [[Photoprotectin (PHPR)]] is expressed, so brightness is a resource with a ceiling rather than a straight gradient of quality.

**Light is not food.** It supplies energy and, through a reaction centre, reducing power — but no carbon. A phototroph still needs organic carbon uptake or the full fixation chain through [[Carbex Permease (CBXP)]] and [[Glycon Synthase (GLYS)]]. Specification §2.4 requires this distinction to be maintained, and [[Metabolic Map]] shows where it bites.

**Direction matters more than intensity.** Because light has a direction, it can serve as a reference frame for [[Localization]] — the basis of requirement V05, where machinery is placed relative to a cue rather than to the cell body. [[Photovector (PHVC)]] is the gene that makes that frame available, and specification §2.1 is explicit that a scalar intensity reading does not automatically supply a directional vector.

**Open:** whether day/night cycling or spectral quality exist at all. Specification §7.1 leaves both undecided, and each would change phototroph balance substantially.
