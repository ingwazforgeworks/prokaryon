**Resource Name:** Biomass  
**Type:** Cell-internal pool — structural material  
**Status:** Confirmed — required by specification §4.2  
**Description:** The cell's physical substance. Biomass is what growth adds, what division splits, what maintenance slowly loses, and what a corpse returns to the world. It is the difference between having energy and having a body.  
**Source:** Assembled by [[Anabolase (ANAB)]] from a carbon source, nitrogen from [[Nitrox Assimilase (NITA)]], and [[ATP]].  
**Uses:** Cell size and growth; the division threshold; maintenance losses; released on death.

**Energy is not material.** A cell with abundant [[ATP]] and no carbon or nitrogen cannot grow. Specification §4.2 keeps the energy balance and the biomass balance as separate equations for exactly this reason, and the split is what makes [[Nitrox]] limitation meaningful rather than cosmetic.

**Division and conservation.** Specification §4.2 warns against accidentally duplicating biomass at division, and §4.4 forbids net matter creation from corpses. Two rules follow:

- Division splits the parent's biomass between daughters; it does not give each a full share.
- Death converts biomass into [[Carbohydron]] and [[Cerumen]] at a loss, so scavenging can never be more profitable than the growth that produced the corpse.
