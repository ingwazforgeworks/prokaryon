**Resource Name:** Oxidex  
**Type:** [[Soluble Resource]] — electron acceptor  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** A small, highly reactive dissolved oxidant. The best place in the world to put an electron, and corrosive to any cell that cannot handle it. It diffuses freely, so uptake is not normally the limiting step.  
**Source:** External: dissolved from the surface, carried inward by [[Flow]], and produced as a byproduct of some phototrophy. Depleted by respiration faster than it is replaced in enclosed spaces.  
**Uses:** Consumed by [[Oxidex Reductase (OXDR)]] to raise the [[ATP]] yield of [[ATP Synthase (ATPS)]]. Detoxified by [[Oxidex Detoxase (OXDT)]].

**Both the prize and the hazard.** Oxidex gives the highest energy yield in the game and simultaneously damages the cell holding it. Exploiting it therefore costs two genes, not one: [[Oxidex Reductase (OXDR)]] to use it and [[Oxidex Detoxase (OXDT)]] to survive it. A lineage that buys only the first will out-grow everyone briefly and then accumulate damage.

**Oxidex draws the map.** Because it is consumed locally and replenished only by transport, its concentration follows enclosure and current rather than depth alone. Open flowing water is rich; a cave interior or the middle of a dense colony is not. This is the mechanism behind [[Redox Stratification]] and the reason [[Sulfex]] respiration has anywhere to live.

**Transport is optional and probably marginal.** Oxidex is small enough to cross a membrane unaided, so gating aerobic life behind a transporter would be a false cost. [[Oxidex Permease (OXDP)]] exists only to raise the *rate ceiling* in oxidant-poor water, and carries an open note recommending it be cut if it cannot be made to work that way.
