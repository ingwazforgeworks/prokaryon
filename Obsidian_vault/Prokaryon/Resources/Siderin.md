**Resource Name:** Siderin  
**Type:** [[Soluble Resource]] — secreted chelator  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** A secreted molecule that binds tightly to mineral iron and carries it into solution. Expensive to make, effective at range, and completely indiscriminate about which cell eventually absorbs what it frees.  
**Source:** Secreted by [[Siderin Synthase (SIDS)]].  
**Uses:** Liberates [[Ferron]] from [[Ferracite]] without requiring surface contact. The freed Ferron is importable by any cell with [[Ferron Permease (FERP)]].

**The clearest public good in the game.** Siderin production costs carbon and energy; its benefit is a soluble nutrient in shared water. A neighbour carrying only [[Ferron Permease (FERP)]] eats exactly as well as the producer and pays nothing. Specification §7.4 lists secreted enzymes benefiting non-producers as the mechanism behind public-goods exploitation, and this is the least ambiguous instance.

**Contrast with the alternative.** [[Ferracite Reductase (FCTR)]] extracts the same [[Ferron]] but only where the cell is physically touching mineral, keeping the product against its own membrane. The two genes are a deliberate pair:

| | Siderin route | Reductase route |
|---|---|---|
| Range | Works at a distance | Contact only |
| Leakage | High | Low |
| Requires attachment | No | Yes, via [[Adhesin (ADHN)]] or [[Pilin (PILN)]] |

A Siderin lineage is mobile and exploitable; a reductase lineage is anchored and defensible.
