**Resource Name:** Quoron  
**Type:** [[Soluble Resource]] — signal molecule  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** A small diffusible molecule cells release continuously and cheaply. It carries no energy and does nothing chemically; its only function is that its concentration reports how many emitters are nearby.  
**Source:** Secreted by [[Quoron Synthase (QURS)]].  
**Uses:** Detected by [[Quoron Receptor (QURR)]] and used as a regulatory input for density-dependent expression.

**Density as an input.** Quoron is how a cell can know something it cannot otherwise measure: that it is in a crowd. That unlocks decisions which only make sense collectively — building [[Matrixin]] when there are enough neighbours to make a biofilm worth having, or releasing [[Lysin]] only when the colony is large enough to survive its own toxin.

**A signal is public.** Quoron diffuses like any other solute, so any species with a receptor can read it. A colony announcing its own density is also advertising itself to [[Bdellase (BDEL)]] predators and to competitors deciding where to invest. Requirement V10 asks for species that help, exploit, or attack one another, and a signal that only ever helped its emitter would not support that.

**Open:** specification §7.1 asks how many independent signal molecules may exist simultaneously. Quoron is currently a single shared channel. Per-species signal identities would let lineages coordinate privately and would multiply the field layers the simulation must carry — see backlog task S3-07 on bounded signal identities and ranges.
