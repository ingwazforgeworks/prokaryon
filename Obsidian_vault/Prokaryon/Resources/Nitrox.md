**Resource Name:** Nitrox  
**Type:** [[Soluble Resource]] — electron donor and nitrogen source  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** A dissolved nitrogenous fuel comprising ammonia, ammonium and nitrite. Released through decomposition and selected metabolic waste pathways, it supports specialists around nutrient-rich sediments and biological colonies.  
**Source:** Released from [[Azoite]] by [[Azoite Hydrolase (AZOH)]]; excreted as waste by other lineages; concentrated in sediment.  
**Uses:** Imported by [[Nitrox Permease (NITP)]]. Burned for energy via [[Fluxidase (FLUX)]], or assimilated into [[Biomass]] by [[Nitrox Assimilase (NITA)]].  
**Strategic role:** The only nitrogen source in the game, which makes it structurally different from every other fuel. A cell can choose which fuel to burn, but it cannot choose to grow without nitrogen. Burning Nitrox for energy therefore competes directly with using it to build, and that internal conflict is the intended decision.
