**Resource Name:** Azoite  
**Type:** [[Insoluble Resource]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Compacted nitrogen-rich organic residue — the settled remains of many generations of cells. It holds the largest nitrogen reserve in most habitats and none of it can be imported directly.  
**Source:** Environmental sediment; accumulates slowly from the [[Biomass]] of dead cells.  
**Uses:** Broken down by [[Azoite Hydrolase (AZOH)]] to release [[Nitrox]].  
**Strategic role:** Azoite is why sediment is worth living in. Because [[Nitrox]] is the only nitrogen source, a habitat whose dissolved nitrogen has been consumed still holds plenty locked in Azoite, and the lineage that pays for the hydrolase unlocks growth for everyone nearby.
