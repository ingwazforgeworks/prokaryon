**Gene ID:** NITP  
**Gene Name:** Nitrox Permease  
**Category:** [[Metabolism]]  
**Status:** Confirmed  
**Description:** Transports [[Nitrox]] into the cell
**Inputs:** [[Nitrox]] (extracellular)  
**Outputs:** [[Nitrox]] (intracellular)  
**Expression scaling:** [[Linear Expression Scaling]]  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None
**Other Prerequisites:** None  
**Default Localization:** [[Plasma Membrane]]  
**Tradeoff:** It imports the one resource with two irreconcilable uses. [[Nitrox]] is both a fuel for [[Fluxidase (FLUX)]] and the only nitrogen source for [[Nitrox Assimilase (NITA)]], [[Chlorin Synthase (CHLS)]] and [[Phycin Synthase (PHYS)]] — so every cell carrying this gene has to choose continuously between burning its nitrogen and building with it, and the permease does nothing to help it choose.
