**Gene ID:** YFP  
**Gene Name:** Yellow Fluorescent Protein  
**Category:** [[Metabolism]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Emits yellow light from chemical energy. The glow is the cell's own, and it is independent of the body colour its pigments give it.  
**Inputs:** [[Nitrox]], a little [[Glycon]], [[ATP]]  
**Outputs:** Yellow [[Light]], local and weak  
**Expression scaling:** [[Linear Expression Scaling]] — brightness and the ATP drain both follow abundance  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None  
**Other Prerequisites:** None  
**Default Localization:** [[Cytosol]]  
**Tradeoff:** Yellow sits in the mid-spectrum band [[Rhodin]] absorbs, and Rhodin is the cheapest sensor in the catalog. A yellow cell is readable by the largest number of sighted lineages, including heterotrophs that bought [[Rhodin Synthase (RHDS)]] only to see. It is the worst colour for going unnoticed, and a standing [[ATP]] cost on top of that.  
**Design basis:** Yellow fluorescent protein, a spectral variant of green fluorescent protein, collapsed here into one module that spends [[ATP]] to emit.

**A glow, not a pigment.** [[Rhodin]], [[Chlorin]], [[Carotin]] and [[Phycin]] colour the body by absorbing [[Light]]. YFP adds yellow light to the water. The two can be carried together, and neither replaces the other. See [[Phototrophy]].
