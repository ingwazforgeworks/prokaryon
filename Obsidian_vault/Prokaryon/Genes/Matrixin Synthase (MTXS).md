**Gene ID:** MTXS  
**Gene Name:** Matrixin Synthase  
**Category:** [[Morphology]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Secretes [[Matrixin]], the polymer that accumulates into a biofilm. Unlike other secretions it does not drift away, so a group expressing it builds a persistent structure with measurably different transport properties from the water around it.  
**Inputs:** [[Glycon]] (carbon), [[ATP]]  
**Outputs:** [[Matrixin]] deposited locally  
**Expression scaling:** [[Linear Expression Scaling]], driven well by a [[Quoron Receptor (QURR)]] density condition  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None  
**Other Prerequisites:** Effective only when enough neighbours also express it; wasted by an isolated cell  
**Default Localization:** Extracellular  
**Tradeoff:** Expensive, collective, and occupiable. One cell's contribution accomplishes nothing, the benefit is shared with anything that drifts inside — including competitors and predators — and the finished structure traps waste as effectively as it traps nutrients.  
**Design basis:** Extracellular polymeric substance and biofilm formation.

**Players building terrain.** This is the main route by which a lineage changes its environment rather than adapting to it. A mature biofilm slows [[Flow]], retains the products of extracellular digestion, and impedes [[Lysin]] diffusing inward — three of the levers specification §7.2 lists for microniche character, generated by play instead of authored.

**It creates its own gradient.** The same impeded transport keeps [[Fermentate]] and acidity inside. A thickening biofilm drifts toward low [[Oxidex]] and low pH at its centre, so the interior of a successful colony becomes a harsher habitat than its surface — and a niche for [[Sulfex Reductase (SLFR)]] lineages that could not otherwise live in open water. See [[Microniche]].

**The threshold makes it a real decision.** Because MTXS is worthless alone and valuable in a crowd, it is the clearest case for density-gated expression: the player sets a [[Quoron]] threshold rather than a constant level, and gets it wrong by building too early.
