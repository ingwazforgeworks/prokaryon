**Gene ID:** SLFP  
**Gene Name:** Sulfex Permease  
**Category:** [[Metabolism]]  
**Status:** Confirmed  
**Description:** Transports [[Sulfex]] into the cell.
**Inputs:** [[Sulfex]] (extracellular)  
**Outputs:** [[Sulfex]] (intracellular)  
**Expression scaling:** [[Linear Expression Scaling]]  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None
**Other Prerequisites:** None  
**Default Localization:** [[Plasma Membrane]]  
**Tradeoff:** The fuel is only where the conditions are hardest. [[Sulfex]] accumulates in dark, enclosed, anoxic water, which is also where [[Oxidex]] respiration is impossible — so a Sulfex lineage is committed to the lowest-yield geography in the game. It also competes for the pool with [[Sulfex Reductase (SLFR)]] cells, which consume the same resource from the other direction.
