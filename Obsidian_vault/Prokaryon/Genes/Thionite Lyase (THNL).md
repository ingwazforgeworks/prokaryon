**Gene ID:** THNL  
**Gene Name:** Thionite Lyase  
**Category:** [[Metabolism]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Dissolves sulfide mineral crust on contact, releasing [[Sulfex]]. Works only against material the cell is physically touching, so it is useless to a drifting cell however strongly it is expressed.  
**Inputs:** [[Thionite]], [[ATP]]  
**Outputs:** [[Sulfex]] (dissolved, at the cell surface)  
**Expression scaling:** [[Saturating Scaling]] — limited by available surface contact, not by enzyme abundance  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None  
**Other Prerequisites:** Physical contact with a [[Thionite]] surface; attachment via [[Adhesin (ADHN)]] or [[Pilin (PILN)]] in any [[Flow]]  
**Default Localization:** Extracellular, surface-facing  
**Tradeoff:** Mines a finite deposit. A colony that succeeds here exhausts its own location and must then solve dispersal with genes it did not buy while it was eating.  
**Design basis:** Acid-mediated mineral dissolution by attached chemolithotrophs. The contact requirement is what makes [[Surfaces and Geometry]] mechanically load-bearing rather than decorative.
