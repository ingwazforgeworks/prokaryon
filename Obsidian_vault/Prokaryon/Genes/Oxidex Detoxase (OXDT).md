**Gene ID:** OXDT  
**Gene Name:** Oxidex Detoxase  
**Category:** [[Homeostasis]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Neutralises the reactive byproducts of [[Oxidex]] before they damage the cell. The gene that makes oxygen-rich water survivable rather than merely profitable.  
**Inputs:** Reactive byproducts; trace [[ATP]]  
**Outputs:** Reduced oxidative damage  
**Expression scaling:** [[Linear Expression Scaling]], driven well by a [[Redoxin (RDXN)]] condition  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None  
**Other Prerequisites:** None  
**Default Localization:** Cytosol  
**Tradeoff:** Pure overhead with no upside. It produces no energy, no material and no capability — it only prevents a loss, which makes it the easiest gene for a new player to under-value and the most punishing to omit.  
**Design basis:** Catalase and superoxide dismutase systems.

**Half of a two-gene purchase.** [[Oxidex]] gives the best energy yield in the game, and damages its user in proportion to how much it uses. A lineage that buys [[Oxidex Reductase (OXDR)]] alone out-grows everything briefly and then accumulates damage faster than [[Repairase (REPR)]] can clear it. Aerobic respiration therefore costs two genes, and the [[Metabolic Map]] yield advantage is priced accordingly.

It matters even without OXDR. Damage tracks ambient [[Oxidex]], not consumption, so any cell living in bright open water pays this tax whether or not it is respiring aerobically — which is part of why shade and enclosure are worth something.
