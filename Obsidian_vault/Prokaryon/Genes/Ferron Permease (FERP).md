**Gene ID:** FERP  
**Gene Name:** Ferron Permease  
**Category:** [[Metabolism]]  
**Status:** Confirmed  
**Description:** Transports [[Ferron]] into the cell for metabolism.
**Inputs:** [[Ferron]] (extracellularly)  
**Outputs:** [[Ferron]] (intracellular)  
**Expression scaling:** [[Linear Expression Scaling]]  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None
**Other Prerequisites:** None  
**Default Localization:** [[Plasma Membrane]]  
**Tradeoff:** Soluble [[Ferron]] barely exists on its own. Almost all of it is locked in [[Ferracite]] or bound to [[Siderin]] somebody else secreted, so this permease is cheap and usually idle — its value comes entirely from pairing with [[Ferracite Reductase (FCTR)]], or from sitting next to a [[Siderin Synthase (SIDS)]] producer and taking what leaks.
