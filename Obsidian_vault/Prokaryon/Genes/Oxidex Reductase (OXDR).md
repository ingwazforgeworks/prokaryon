**Gene ID:** OXDR  
**Gene Name:** Oxidex Reductase  
**Category:** [[Metabolism]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** A terminal respiratory module. Passes spent electrons to [[Oxidex]], which multiplies the [[ATP]] that [[ATP Synthase (ATPS)]] extracts from each unit of [[Fluxin]] and suppresses [[Fermentate]] excretion. The single largest economic upgrade in the game.  
**Inputs:** [[Oxidex]] (extracellular, diffuses in freely)  
**Outputs:** Raised ATP yield per [[Fluxin]]; oxidative damage as a side effect  
**Expression scaling:** [[Saturating Scaling]] — capped by local [[Oxidex]] availability, not by expression  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** [[ATP Synthase (ATPS)]]  
**Other Prerequisites:** Local [[Oxidex]]; survival at high yield effectively requires [[Oxidex Detoxase (OXDT)]]  
**Default Localization:** Cell membrane  
**Tradeoff:** Ties the lineage to open, flowing, exposed water and generates damage in proportion to its own success. It is dead weight in caves, sediment, and the interior of a thick biofilm.  
**Design basis:** Aerobic respiration with a terminal oxidase. See [[Metabolic Map]] for the yield table and [[Redox Stratification]] for why the requirement draws the map.
