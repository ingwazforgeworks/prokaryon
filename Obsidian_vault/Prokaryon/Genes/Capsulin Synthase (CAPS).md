**Gene ID:** CAPS  
**Gene Name:** Capsulin Synthase  
**Category:** [[Morphology]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Lays down a [[Capsulin]] polymer layer on the cell's own surface. Protection that travels with the cell and shelters nobody else.  
**Inputs:** [[Glycon]] (carbon), [[ATP]]  
**Outputs:** [[Capsulin]] bound to the cell surface  
**Expression scaling:** [[Linear Expression Scaling]]  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None  
**Other Prerequisites:** None  
**Default Localization:** Cell membrane, outward-facing  
**Tradeoff:** Costs carbon that could have been [[Biomass]], slows uptake like any added surface layer, and is lost entirely when the cell dies. A capsule is rent, not property.  
**Design basis:** Polysaccharide capsules and glycocalyx layers.

**Private versus public protection.** CAPS is the deliberate counterpart to [[Matrixin Synthase (MTXS)]]. Both cost carbon, both defend against [[Bdellase (BDEL)]] and [[Lysin]], and they differ only in who benefits — which turns a defensive choice into an economic one. See [[Capsulin]] for the full comparison.

A lineage under predation pressure can pay alone and reliably, or pay less inside a biofilm that free-riders can also occupy. Specification §7.4 asks what makes public-goods exploitation interesting; offering a private alternative at a higher price is what gives the player a reason to think about it.
