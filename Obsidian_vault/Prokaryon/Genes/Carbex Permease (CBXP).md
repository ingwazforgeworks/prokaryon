**Gene ID:** CBXP  
**Gene Name:** Carbex Permease  
**Category:** [[Metabolism]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Concentrates dissolved inorganic carbon inside the cell. [[Carbex]] is abundant almost everywhere but too dilute to fix at a useful rate, so this transporter exists to raise the internal concentration rather than to gain access to something unavailable.  
**Inputs:** [[Carbex]] (extracellular), [[ATP]]  
**Outputs:** [[Carbex]] (intracellular, concentrated)  
**Expression scaling:** [[Saturating Scaling]]  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None  
**Other Prerequisites:** Useless without [[Glycon Synthase (GLYS)]] and a [[Reducin]] supply  
**Default Localization:** Cell membrane  
**Tradeoff:** Active transport against a gradient costs [[ATP]] continuously, and the resource it imports is worthless without three other genes.  
**Design basis:** Inorganic carbon concentrating mechanisms. The gate into autotrophy, positioned so that fixation is a deliberate multi-gene investment as specification §21.4 requires.
