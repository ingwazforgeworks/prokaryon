**Gene ID:** CRTS  
**Gene Name:** Carotin Synthase  
**Category:** [[Metabolism]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Builds [[Carotin]], the pigment that harvests blue light and safely discards energy the cell cannot use. Both an antenna and a safety valve.  
**Inputs:** [[Glycon]] or [[Lipron]] (carbon), [[ATP]]  
**Outputs:** [[Carotin]]  
**Expression scaling:** [[Linear Expression Scaling]]  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None  
**Other Prerequisites:** Required by [[Photoprotectin (PHPR)]]; contributes to [[Reaction Center (RXNC)]] as an accessory antenna  
**Default Localization:** [[Cytosol]] and [[Plasma Membrane]]  
**Tradeoff:** Carotin is a poor antenna and an imperfect shield, so it is never the best choice for either job. Its value is entirely in doing both at once, which makes it easy to under-invest in and hard to over-invest in.  
**Design basis:** Carotenoid biosynthesis, supporting both accessory light harvesting and non-photochemical quenching.

**It has no clean optimum.** Every other pigment gets worse past a point — more [[Chlorin]] means more photodamage. More Carotin means more harvest *and* more protection, so the curve does not turn over. The limit is material cost alone, which makes this the one pigment where a player can simply buy more and be right.

**It is nitrogen-free.** Unlike [[Chlorin]] and [[Phycin]], Carotin needs no [[Nitrox]], so it remains affordable in exactly the nitrogen-poor water where the other pigments become unaffordable. A surface lineage in poor water ends up carotin-dominated — visibly warm-coloured, moderately productive, and hard to kill with [[Light]].
