**Gene ID:** RHDS  
**Gene Name:** Rhodin Synthase  
**Category:** [[Metabolism]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Builds [[Rhodin]], the cheapest pigment in the game. One small molecule that can either drive a light pump directly or give a [[Photochrome (PCHR)]] receptor its mid-spectrum sensitivity.  
**Inputs:** [[Lipron]] or [[Glycon]] (carbon), [[ATP]]  
**Outputs:** [[Rhodin]]  
**Expression scaling:** [[Linear Expression Scaling]]  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None  
**Other Prerequisites:** Useless without [[Photorhodin (PHOR)]] or [[Photochrome (PCHR)]] to use it  
**Default Localization:** [[Cytosol]]  
**Tradeoff:** Cheap because it is limited. Rhodin cannot feed a [[Reaction Center (RXNC)]] well enough to produce [[Reducin]], so a Rhodin-only lineage has a permanent ceiling and can never reach autotrophy on this pigment alone.  
**Design basis:** Retinal biosynthesis supporting rhodopsin-based phototrophy, source [B7].

**The entry point into light.** Two cheap genes — this and [[Photorhodin (PHOR)]] — give a heterotroph a partial energy subsidy in lit water with no other changes. That makes Rhodin the right first purchase for a lineage experimenting with [[Phototrophy]] and the wrong one for a lineage committing to it.

**It is also a sensor pigment.** Because pigments are the prerequisite for light sensing, Rhodin is the cheapest route to any [[Photochrome (PCHR)]] at all — so a lineage that only wants to *detect* light, in order to avoid it or to time [[Photoprotectin (PHPR)]], buys this gene rather than a reaction-centre pigment.
