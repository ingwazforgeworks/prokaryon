**Gene ID:** GRNS  
**Gene Name:** Granulin Synthase  
**Category:** [[Metabolism]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Polymerises surplus intracellular [[Glycon]] into insoluble [[Granulin]] granules. Converts food the cell cannot use now into food it can reach later, at a loss.  
**Inputs:** [[Glycon]] (intracellular), [[ATP]]  
**Outputs:** [[Granulin]]  
**Expression scaling:** [[Linear Expression Scaling]], driven well by a [[Chemoreceptor (CHMR)]] condition on abundant food  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None  
**Other Prerequisites:** Pointless without [[Granulin Hydrolase (GRNH)]] to recover the store  
**Default Localization:** Cytosol  
**Tradeoff:** Both conversions waste material, so a cell that stores and retrieves ends up poorer than one that never stored. Granules also occupy internal volume, making a well-provisioned cell larger and slower without bringing it closer to [[Division]].  
**Design basis:** Glycogen and polyhydroxyalkanoate storage granule synthesis.

**A bet, not an upgrade.** Storage only pays across a real famine, and the player places the bet by choosing a promoter condition rather than by acting at the right moment. That is the distinction requirement V02 depends on: the decision is genetic and made in advance, not reactive.

**It subsidises predators.** Stored [[Granulin]] returns to the world as [[Carbohydron]] when its owner dies, so a full larder makes a richer corpse. [[Bdellase (BDEL)]] lineages should prefer well-fed prey for exactly that reason — the one case in the catalog where investing in survival makes the investor a better target.
