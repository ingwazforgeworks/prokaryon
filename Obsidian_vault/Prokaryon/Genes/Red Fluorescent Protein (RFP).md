**Gene ID:** RFP  
**Gene Name:** Red Fluorescent Protein  
**Category:** [[Metabolism]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Emits red light from chemical energy. The glow is the cell's own, and it is independent of the body colour its pigments give it.  
**Inputs:** [[Nitrox]], a little [[Glycon]], [[ATP]]  
**Outputs:** Red [[Light]], local and weak  
**Expression scaling:** [[Linear Expression Scaling]] — brightness and the ATP drain both follow abundance  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None  
**Other Prerequisites:** None  
**Default Localization:** [[Cytosol]]  
**Tradeoff:** The cheapest of the four emissions, and the band [[Chlorin]] is built to take. In the dark a red cell feeds every Chlorin neighbour in range and tells every Chlorin-sighted cell where it is. In sunlit water the environmental red swamps the glow, and the ATP is spent on light nobody can pick out.  
**Design basis:** Red fluorescent protein (DsRed and its relatives). The real proteins re-emit absorbed light; this module spends [[ATP]] to emit, so the cell glows in the dark, with the fluorescent protein supplying the colour.

**A glow, not a pigment.** [[Rhodin]], [[Chlorin]], [[Carotin]] and [[Phycin]] colour the body by absorbing [[Light]]. RFP adds red light to the water. The two can be carried together, and neither replaces the other. See [[Phototrophy]].
