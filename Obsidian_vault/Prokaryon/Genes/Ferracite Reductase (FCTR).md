**Gene ID:** FCTR  
**Gene Name:** Ferracite Reductase  
**Category:** [[Metabolism]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Reduces mineral iron in place at the point of contact, freeing [[Ferron]] directly against the cell's own membrane. The slow, anchored, defensible route to mineral iron.  
**Inputs:** [[Ferracite]], [[Fluxin]]  
**Outputs:** [[Ferron]] (at the cell surface)  
**Expression scaling:** [[Saturating Scaling]] — limited by contact area  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** None  
**Other Prerequisites:** Sustained contact with [[Ferracite]]; requires [[Ferron Permease (FERP)]] to be worth anything  
**Default Localization:** Cell membrane, surface-facing  
**Tradeoff:** Demands immobility. The cell must attach and stay, forfeiting every strategy that depends on going somewhere, and it spends [[Fluxin]] to obtain a fuel that becomes Fluxin.  
**Design basis:** Dissimilatory metal reduction at a mineral interface. The deliberate counterpart to [[Siderin Synthase (SIDS)]]: same resource, opposite exposure profile — see [[Siderin]] for the comparison.
