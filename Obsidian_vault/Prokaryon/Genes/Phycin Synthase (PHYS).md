**Gene ID:** PHYS  
**Gene Name:** Phycin Synthase  
**Category:** [[Metabolism]]  
**Status:** Proposed  
**Description:** Builds [[Phycin]], a large accessory antenna that harvests the green and orange light [[Chlorin]] leaves behind. The specialist pigment for living in somebody else's shadow.  
**Inputs:** [[Glycon]] (carbon), [[Nitrox]] (substantial — a protein-pigment assembly), [[ATP]]  
**Outputs:** [[Phycin]]  
**Expression scaling:** [[Linear Expression Scaling]]  
**Genetic Prerequisites:** [[Nitrox Permease (NITP)]]  
**Other Prerequisites:** [[Reaction Center (RXNC)]]  
**Default Localization:** [[Cytosol]]  
**Tradeoff:** The highest material cost in [[Phototrophy]] for the poorest light available. In fresh full-spectrum water it is simply worse than [[Chlorin]] — more nitrogen, more bulk, less energy.  
**Design basis:** Phycobilisome antenna assembly and complementary chromatic adaptation.

**Its habitat does not exist until another player makes it.** Phycin becomes correct only beneath an established [[Chlorin]] population that has already stripped out the red and blue. That makes it the clearest entry in the catalog whose viability is set by the ecology rather than the environment — and the reason it should never be balanced against the world, only against other lineages.

Specification §7.4's emergence gate asks for two different outcomes from the same rules under different conditions, with the mechanism explained. A Phycin lineage that fails in open water and thrives under a competitor's canopy is that demonstration, and the mechanism is light absorption, not a rule about competitors.
