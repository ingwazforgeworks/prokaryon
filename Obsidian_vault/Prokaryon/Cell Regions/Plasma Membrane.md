**Region:** Plasma Membrane  
**Status:** Confirmed

The boundary. Everything that moves a resource across it, senses the outside world, or touches another cell is anchored here.

**What lives here:** transporters, channels, terminal reductases, surface receptors, appendages, contact machinery, surface armour

**Genes:** [[Glycon Permease (GLYP)]] · [[Lipron Permease (LIPP)]] · [[Nitrox Permease (NITP)]] · [[Sulfex Permease (SLFP)]] · [[Ferron Permease (FERP)]] · [[Carbex Permease (CBXP)]] · [[Oxidex Permease (OXDP)]] · [[Fermentate Permease (FRMP)]] · [[Siderin Permease (SIDP)]] · [[Oxidex Reductase (OXDR)]] · [[Sulfex Reductase (SLFR)]] · [[Ferracite Reductase (FCTR)]] · [[Photorhodin (PHOR)]] · [[Reaction Center (RXNC)]] · [[Photoprotectin (PHPR)]] · [[Protopump (PPMP)]] · [[Aquaporin (AQUP)]] · [[Membrane Saturase (MSAT)]] · [[Membrane Desaturase (MDES)]] · [[Envelope Thickener (ENVT)]] · [[Capsulin Synthase (CAPS)]] · [[Adhesin (ADHN)]] · [[Cohesin (COHS)]] · [[Pilin (PILN)]] · [[Flagellar Motor Protein (FLGM)]] · [[Flagellin (FLGN)]] · [[Cilium Assemblase (CILA)]] · [[Ciliary Motor (CILM)]] · [[Ciliary Polarizer (CILP)]] · [[Ciliary Reversal Channel (CILR)]] · [[Ciliary Calcium Pump (CILC)]] · [[Effector Injector (EFFI)]] · [[Bdellase (BDEL)]] · [[Envelope Defensin (DEFN)]] · [[Conjugation Apparatus (CONJ)]] · [[Competence Uptake (COMP)]] · [[Lysin Resistase (LYSR)]] · [[Chemoreceptor (CHMR)]] · [[Photochrome (PCHR)]] · [[Photovector (PHVC)]] · [[Osmoreceptor (OSMR)]] · [[Quoron Receptor (QURR)]] · [[Quoron Synthase (QURS)]]

**It is a contested budget, not just a place.** Membrane area is finite, so transporters, receptors, motors and armour compete for surface in a way cytosolic proteins do not. This is what makes the permeability tradeoff in [[Morphology]] real: [[Envelope Thickener (ENVT)]] does not merely cost material, it occupies the boundary every permease needs.

**It is also where placement becomes a decision.** Membrane proteins have a position, which is why [[Localization]] and requirement V05 apply here and nowhere else. [[PolarLocalizationSignal]] acts from the [[Cytosol]] on this region.
