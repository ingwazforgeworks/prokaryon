**Region:** Cytosol  
**Status:** Confirmed

The cell's interior fluid. Everything expressed here acts on intracellular pools and is unreachable by other cells until this one dies.

**What lives here:** conversion enzymes, biosynthesis, regulators, receptors reading internal state

**Genes:** [[Fluxidase (FLUX)]] · [[ATP Synthase (ATPS)]] · [[Reducin Dehydrogenase (RDCD)]] · [[Glycon Synthase (GLYS)]] · [[Nitrox Assimilase (NITA)]] · [[Anabolase (ANAB)]] · [[Granulin Synthase (GRNS)]] · [[Granulin Hydrolase (GRNH)]] · [[Repressor (REPX)]] · [[Activator (ACTX)]] · [[Memory Latch (MEML)]] · [[Repairase (REPR)]] · [[Oxidex Detoxase (OXDT)]] · [[Heat Shock Protein (HSP)]] · [[Cold Shock Protein (CSP)]] · [[Osmolyn Synthase (OSMS)]] · [[Taxis Regulator (TAXR)]] · [[Buoyin (BUOY)]] · [[Floatin (FLOA)]] · [[Ballastin (BALA)]] · [[Chlorin Synthase (CHLS)]] · [[Rhodin Synthase (RHDS)]] · [[Carotin Synthase (CRTS)]] · [[Phycin Synthase (PHYS)]] · [[Quoron Synthase (QURS)]] · [[Sporulase (SPOR)]] · [[Plasmid Maintainer (PLSM)]] · [[Restriction Defense (RSTD)]] · [[Protoreceptor (PRTR)]] · [[Thermoreceptor (THMR)]] · [[Redoxin (RDXN)]] · [[Damage Receptor (DMGR)]]

**Why the region matters.** Cytosolic products are private. Everything in [[Extracellular]] is contestable, and everything on the [[Plasma Membrane]] is exposed. That difference is the entire basis of the public-goods problem in [[Morphology]] — the same enzyme is a competitive advantage inside and a gift outside.
