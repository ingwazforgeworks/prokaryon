**Region:** Extracellular  
**Status:** Confirmed

Outside the cell entirely. Anything released here is gone: it diffuses, is carried by [[Flow]], and is available to every other cell nearby.

**What lives here:** digestive enzymes, secreted polymers, signals, toxins, chelators

**Genes:** [[Glycon Hydrolase (GLYH)]] · [[Lipron Hydrolase (LIPH)]] · [[Azoite Hydrolase (AZOH)]] · [[Thionite Lyase (THNL)]] · [[Siderin Synthase (SIDS)]] · [[Matrixin Synthase (MTXS)]] · [[Lysin Synthase (LYSS)]] — each of them only if tagged with [[SecretoryPeptide]]

**The region that creates the game's social problems.** Every gene in this list pays a private cost to produce a public benefit. Specification §7.4 requires public-goods exploitation to arise from a declared mechanism rather than a rule, and this region *is* that mechanism: the product simply appears somewhere its producer does not control.

**The only defence is to stop using it.** [[TransmembraneSignal]] tethers enzymes back to the [[Plasma Membrane]], trading range for retention, and [[Cohesin (COHS)]] keeps a producer's neighbours the same type. Both are the same move — shrinking the extracellular space that matters.

**Note on [[Capsulin]].** Capsule polymer is produced for the extracellular side but stays bound to its producer, which is why [[Capsulin Synthase (CAPS)]] is localized to the [[Plasma Membrane]] rather than here. The contrast with [[Matrixin]], which is genuinely released, is deliberate.
