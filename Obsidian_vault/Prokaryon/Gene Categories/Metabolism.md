**Category:** Metabolism  
**Status:** Confirmed as a content family — specification §6.4  
**Genes:** 35 — the largest category by a wide margin

Everything that acquires material or energy, converts one form into another, or stores it. If a gene changes what is in the cell's pools, it lives here.

### The four jobs

| Job | Function words | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Get it inside | Permease | [[Glycon Permease (GLYP)]], [[Nitrox Permease (NITP)]], [[Carbex Permease (CBXP)]] |
| Break it down | Hydrolase, Lyase | [[Glycon Hydrolase (GLYH)]], [[Azoite Hydrolase (AZOH)]], [[Thionite Lyase (THNL)]] |
| Extract energy | Reductase, Dehydrogenase | [[Oxidex Reductase (OXDR)]], [[Sulfex Reductase (SLFR)]], [[ATP Synthase (ATPS)]] |
| Build and store | Synthase, Assimilase | [[Anabolase (ANAB)]], [[Glycon Synthase (GLYS)]], [[Granulin Synthase (GRNS)]] |

Most viable genomes need at least one gene from each row. A cell with uptake and no breakdown starves holding food; one with breakdown and no uptake feeds its neighbours.

### The load-bearing decision

Which terminal reductase a cell expresses sets how much [[ATP]] it gets per unit of [[Fluxin]] — and each option is only available in certain water. That single coupling is what makes [[Redox Stratification]] the most consequential geography in the game:

| Terminal module | Yield | Requires | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| None — fermentation | Lowest | Nothing | Excretes [[Fermentate]], acidifying its own water |
| [[Sulfex Reductase (SLFR)]] | Moderate | [[Sulfex]] | Anoxic water only |
| [[Oxidex Reductase (OXDR)]] | Highest | [[Oxidex]] | Oxidative damage; needs [[Oxidex Detoxase (OXDT)]] |

See [[Metabolic Map]] for the full flow and [[Resource Index]] for the pools.

### What was folded in here

**Phototrophy.** [[Photorhodin (PHOR)]] and [[Reaction Center (RXNC)]] are energy acquisition, so they belong with the rest of it. The pigment synthases — [[Chlorin Synthase (CHLS)]], [[Rhodin Synthase (RHDS)]], [[Carotin Synthase (CRTS)]], [[Phycin Synthase (PHYS)]] — are here too, though their products also gate [[Perception]] through [[Photochrome (PCHR)]].

**Light emission.** [[Red Fluorescent Protein (RFP)]], [[Green Fluorescent Protein (GFP)]], [[Yellow Fluorescent Protein (YFP)]] and [[Blue Fluorescent Protein (BFP)]] run the conversion the other way: [[ATP]] out as [[Light]] in one band. They sit here because they spend a pool to change the energy field around the cell. The pigment genes colour the body; these four make it glow.

**Storage.** [[Granulin Synthase (GRNS)]] and [[Granulin Hydrolase (GRNH)]] are conversions in and out of a pool, not a survival mechanism in their own right.

**Iron scavenging.** [[Siderin Synthase (SIDS)]] and [[Siderin Permease (SIDP)]] are acquisition genes that happen to raise a public-goods problem. The problem is interesting; the genes are still metabolism.
