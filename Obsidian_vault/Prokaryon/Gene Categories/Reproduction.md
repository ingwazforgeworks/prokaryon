**Category:** Reproduction  
**Status:** Confirmed as a content family — specification §6.4; the transfer rules are open under decisions D19–D25  
**Genes:** 6

Everything concerning how the genome is copied, protected, passed on, or put on hold. This category governs the game's progression currency, because [[Division]] is how a species earns [[Mutation Points]] under confirmed requirement V08.

### The genes

| Gene | Role |
|---|---|
| [[Conjugation Apparatus (CONJ)]] | Sends a gene to another cell by direct contact |
| [[Competence Uptake (COMP)]] | Takes up genes from dead cells in the water |
| [[Plasmid Maintainer (PLSM)]] | Keeps an acquired gene across divisions |
| [[Restriction Defense (RSTD)]] | Refuses incoming foreign DNA |
| [[Sporulase (SPOR)]] | Suspends the cell until conditions improve |
| [[Cyclin (CYCL)]] | Commits the cell to division early, while expressed |

### Division is the economy

A lineage that divides faster earns edits faster. That makes every metabolic and homeostatic decision in the genome ultimately a decision about division rate, and it is why the categories are not independent — [[Metabolism]] funds [[Reproduction]], which funds everything.

[[Cyclin (CYCL)]] is the gene that spends that rate on purpose. While it is expressed, the cell commits to [[Division]] before the [[Size Regulator (SIZR)]] target, and the daughters are smaller for it. It is Open for the same reward reason as SIZR: a flat payment per division would make early division a currency printer.

The open questions are economic rather than biological: decisions D13–D16 cover how points accrue, whether they pool per species or per cell, and what stops a runaway lineage from editing itself out of reach. See [[Mutation Points]] and [[Division]].

### Gene transfer is the multiplayer mechanic

Confirmed requirement V12 establishes interplayer gene transfer, and this is where it lives. Two routes, deliberately different:

- **Contact** — [[Conjugation Apparatus (CONJ)]] is directed and deliberate. The sender chooses. Source [B3] describes T4SS conjugation, and specification §2.1 insists this is not an injectisome: there is no shared progression edge with [[Effector Injector (EFFI)]].
- **Scavenging** — [[Competence Uptake (COMP)]] is undirected and opportunistic. The recipient takes what happens to be there, and cannot choose what it gets.

[[Restriction Defense (RSTD)]] is the refusal. A cell that takes everything accumulates genes it cannot afford to express; a cell that takes nothing never benefits from anyone else's work. Neither extreme is correct, which is the intended shape.

Decisions D19–D25 remain unresolved, including whether transfer needs consent, whether it can be hostile, and whether a received gene arrives with its [[Gene Modification]] tags. See [[Horizontal Gene Transfer]].

### Persistence is reproductive deferral

[[Sporulase (SPOR)]] is here rather than in [[Homeostasis]] because a spore is not a cell surviving conditions — it is a lineage declining to live in them. It stops metabolising, stops dividing, and stops earning.

That makes dormancy the one strategy that trades progression for survival outright, and the reason it must be costly enough to be a real decision. A free spore makes [[Extinction]] impossible, and decision D06 has not yet fixed where the extinction boundary sits.
