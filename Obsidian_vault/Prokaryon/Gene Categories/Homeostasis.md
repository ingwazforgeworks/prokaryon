**Category:** Homeostasis  
**Status:** Confirmed as a content family — specification §6.4  
**Genes:** 13

Everything that keeps the inside of the cell survivable while the outside changes. These genes rarely produce anything; they prevent losses, and their value is invisible until conditions move.

### The three cost shapes

Homeostasis genes are the design's clearest demonstration that different defences fail differently, and the distinction is worth preserving in every gene here:

| Shape | Behaviour | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Shift the optimum | Better in one direction, worse in the other | [[Membrane Saturase (MSAT)]], [[Membrane Desaturase (MDES)]] |
| Widen the range | Survivable everywhere, optimal nowhere | [[Heat Shock Protein (HSP)]], [[Cold Shock Protein (CSP)]] |
| Pay continuously | Constant upkeep for a constant benefit | [[Acid Tolerase (ACDT)]], [[Protopump (PPMP)]], [[Osmolyn Synthase (OSMS)]] |

A specialist that shifts beats a generalist that widens inside its range and dies outside it. That asymmetry is what keeps both strategies alive, and it is the reason [[Temperature]] and [[pH]] produce distinct populations rather than one tolerant one.

### What it defends against

Each environmental field in [[Environment]] has at least one gene that answers it, which is the first of the three catalog rules in the [[Gene Catalog]]:

- [[Temperature]] — [[Membrane Saturase (MSAT)]], [[Membrane Desaturase (MDES)]], [[Heat Shock Protein (HSP)]], [[Cold Shock Protein (CSP)]]
- [[pH]] — [[Acid Tolerase (ACDT)]], [[Protopump (PPMP)]]
- [[Salinity]] — [[Osmolyn Synthase (OSMS)]], [[Aquaporin (AQUP)]]
- [[Light]] — [[Photoprotectin (PHPR)]]
- [[Oxidex]] — [[Oxidex Detoxase (OXDT)]]
- Attack — [[Lysin Resistase (LYSR)]], [[Envelope Defensin (DEFN)]]
- Accumulated damage — [[Repairase (REPR)]]

### Why defence genes are here and not in a category of their own

[[Lysin Resistase (LYSR)]] and [[Envelope Defensin (DEFN)]] answer a hostile neighbour rather than a hostile environment, but they do the same *kind* of work as every other gene in this category: continuous upkeep to prevent a loss. Splitting them out would create a category defined by what the threat is rather than what the gene does.

Confirmed requirement V29 asks for multiple hostile mechanisms, and it is satisfied by there being several attacks — [[Lysin Synthase (LYSS)]], [[Effector Injector (EFFI)]], [[Bdellase (BDEL)]] — not by the defences being filed separately.

### The hardest thing to get right

A homeostasis gene the player always wants is a tax, not a decision. Every gene here must be genuinely wrong somewhere: [[Photoprotectin (PHPR)]] wastes harvest in dim water, [[Aquaporin (AQUP)]] becomes a liability when [[Salinity]] swings, [[Protopump (PPMP)]] burns [[ATP]] a cell in neutral water needs elsewhere.

Where that condition cannot be stated, the gene should be cut rather than tuned — the same test applied to [[Oxidex Permease (OXDP)]].
